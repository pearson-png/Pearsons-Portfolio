Exploring the Spatiotemporal Relationship Between Marine Species Richness and Sea Temperature

This multi-year, ongoing project has been a deep dive into statistically representing where you could find the largest variety of fish at any point in time off of the United States' east coast. The goal is to parametize the movement high-diversity areas through time and space so we can predict which areas would be most benefial for marine protections as climate change progresses and and ecosystems further destabalize.

Analysis of Wellesley College's Waste Management

I was the lead data editor for my bachelor capstone project analyzing and recommending improvements for our college's municipal waste management. In addition to being a project manager, I applied for and recieved a grant for our lifecycle analysis software, designed the waste audit's data collection, and extrapolated a year's worth of total waste tonnage from the findings.
